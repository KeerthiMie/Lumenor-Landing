How Lumenor Works

Digital Backbone of Solar

Lumenor brings customers, EPC companies and installers onto a single platform.

Customers can discover solar solutions, request quotations and follow their project progress.

EPC teams manage surveys, designs, approvals and project execution through FlowX.

Installers receive tasks, update job status and verify completed work using FixX.

Instead of multiple calls, spreadsheets and disconnected updates, everyone works together through one connected workflow.
